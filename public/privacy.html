01

本地数据

TradeReplay 的核心数据库保存在你的电脑中。模拟训练、真实账单、交易系统、策略扫描结果、日志、截图、自定义指标和软件设置默认不会上传给我们。

删除记录、切换资料库或卸载软件时，数据由你在本机管理。请自行保留必要的本地或 S3 备份。

Local data

TradeReplay stores its core database on your computer. Practice sessions, imported statements, trading systems, scan results, journals, screenshots, custom indicators, and settings are not uploaded to us by default.

You control deletion, library changes, and uninstallation locally. Keep any local or S3 backups you need.

02

激活与购买

激活专业版时，软件会向 TradeReplay 授权服务发送激活码、邮箱、随机生成的设备标识和设备名称，用于验证授权、限制激活设备数量和处理设备解绑。

淘宝或 WHOP 负责处理付款。我们不会保存你的银行卡信息；为交付激活码和处理售后，可能接收订单号、购买邮箱、商品与付款状态。

License activation and purchase

When you activate Pro, the app sends the license code, email address, a randomly generated device identifier, and a device label to the TradeReplay license service. This verifies access, enforces the device limit, and supports device deactivation.

Taobao or Whop processes payment. We do not store card details, but may receive an order ID, purchase email, product, and payment status to deliver the license and provide support.

03

行情下载

下载系统行情时，请求会发送到我们的内容分发服务。服务商可能处理你的 IP 地址、请求的文件和请求时间，用于传输、安全与故障排查。我们不使用这些记录建立交易画像。

Market data downloads

System market data is delivered through our content infrastructure. The provider may process your IP address, requested file, and request time for delivery, security, and troubleshooting. We do not use these records to build a trading profile.

04

自有云备份

只有在你主动配置 S3、COS、R2、MinIO 或其他 S3 兼容存储后，TradeReplay 才会把备份发送到你选择的服务商。连接信息保存在本机设备设置中，密钥优先使用系统安全存储加密。

备份内容、保存期限和云端访问权限由你及所选服务商控制。

User-owned cloud backup

TradeReplay uploads backups only after you configure S3, R2, MinIO, or another S3-compatible service. Connection settings stay in local device settings, and secret keys use operating-system secure storage where available.

You and your selected provider control the backup content, retention, and cloud access.

05

Mentor AI

Mentor AI 使用你提供的 API Key。启用后，你的问题以及软件为分析准备的交易统计或交易上下文会直接发送给你选择的 AI 服务商，不经过 TradeReplay 服务器。

API Key 保存在系统安全存储中。AI 服务商如何处理数据，适用其自己的隐私政策；你可以随时删除 Key 或停用 AI。

Mentor AI

Mentor AI uses an API key you provide. When enabled, your question and the trading statistics or trade context prepared for analysis go directly to your selected AI provider, not through a TradeReplay server.

The API key is kept in operating-system secure storage. Your provider's privacy policy applies to its processing, and you can delete the key or stop using AI at any time.

06

网站与支持

官网不要求注册账户，也不放置广告。我们使用 GoatCounter 进行不依赖 Cookie 的基础访问统计，用于了解页面访问量、来源、浏览器类别、设备尺寸和大致地区，并在官网公开显示累计访问次数。GoatCounter 不使用持久用户标识；统计请求由 GoatCounter 处理，适用其隐私政策。

托管与安全服务可能按其基础设施规则处理访问日志。我们使用汇总统计改进页面内容、兼容性和加载性能，不用于建立个人交易或广告画像。

当你通过邮件、QQ、企业微信、淘宝或 WHOP 联系我们时，我们会处理你主动提供的联系方式、订单信息和问题内容，用于售后与问题解决。

Website and support

The website does not require an account or display advertising. We use GoatCounter for cookie-free, basic traffic analytics, including page views, referrers, browser category, viewport size, and approximate region, and publicly display the aggregate visit count. GoatCounter does not use persistent user identifiers; its privacy policy applies to analytics requests.

Hosting and security providers may process access logs under their infrastructure policies. We use aggregate analytics to improve content, compatibility, and loading performance, not to build personal trading or advertising profiles.

If you contact us by email, QQ, WeCom, Taobao, or Whop, we process the contact details, order information, and issue content you provide to deliver support.

07

你的控制权

  • 在软件中删除或导出本地数据。
  • 删除 AI Key、S3 凭据或停用相应功能。
  • 通过设备解绑释放专业版激活名额。
  • 联系 zclk9000@me.com 查询或删除我们持有的支持与授权联系信息；法律要求保留的交易记录除外。

Your controls

  • Delete or export local data in the app.
  • Remove AI keys or S3 credentials, or stop using those features.
  • Deactivate a device to release a Pro activation seat.
  • Contact zclk9000@me.com to request access to or deletion of support and license contact information we hold, except records we must retain by law.
