Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TradeReplay ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use the TradeReplay desktop application. We have designed TradeReplay as a local-first tool: your trading records remain on your computer unless you explicitly use an export, backup, AI, licensing, or other network feature described below.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect or host your trading records, journal notes, strategy definitions, screenshots, or backtest results. The app does not include advertising identifiers or third-party tracking SDKs. We process limited purchase and license information only when needed to deliver and verify a license, as described in Sections 4.3 and 4.4.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a private SQLite database that only TradeReplay can access:

  • Trading sessions you create within the app, including positions, closed trades, notes, tags, and screenshots you attach.
  • Trade journal entries imported from broker statements (such as MT4, MT5, or CFMMC files) that you choose to import.
  • App preferences, layout choices, and indicator configurations.
  • Strategy definitions and backtest results you generate within the app.

This data does not leave your device unless you explicitly export it or use a network feature described in this policy.

3. API Keys (Optional, Bring-Your-Own-Key AI)

If you choose to use an optional AI feature, you may enter your own API key from a third-party AI provider (such as DeepSeek or OpenAI). This key is stored using your operating system's secure credential storage and is never sent to our servers.

When you trigger an AI summary, the app sends a request directly from your device to the AI provider you selected, using your API key. The request contains a structured summary of your trading statistics (such as win rate, profit factor, and trade count) so the provider can generate analysis. We do not proxy, log, or have access to this request.

You may remove your API key at any time in the app's Settings, which immediately disables the AI feature and deletes the key from your keychain.

4. Third-Party Services

The app communicates with the following external services on your behalf. Each is described below.

4.1 Historical Market Data

To populate sample charts and let you replay historical price data, the app downloads market data files from our content delivery network. These requests transmit only the standard information any web browser sends: your IP address, the requested file path, and the time of the request. We do not associate this information with any user identity, and the data files do not contain any user-specific content.

4.2 AI Provider (Only If You Enable AI)

If you enter an AI provider API key as described in Section 3, requests are sent directly from your device to that provider's API endpoint. The data transmitted consists of the trading statistics summary text plus your API key (used only for authentication with the provider).

The privacy practices of these providers are governed by their own policies:

  • DeepSeek: https://platform.deepseek.com/privacy
  • OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

4.3 License Activation

When you activate TradeReplay Pro, the app sends the purchase email, activation code, a randomly generated device identifier, the computer name and operating system used as a device label, and activation or last-seen timestamps to our license service. We use this information only to verify the license, enforce the device limit, release device slots, prevent abuse, and provide activation support. Trading records are not included.

The license service is hosted using Cloudflare Workers and D1. Cloudflare's privacy policy is available at https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/.

4.4 Purchase and Fulfilment Services

Purchases may be processed by the third-party sales channel shown on the official TradeReplay website. That channel processes payment information under its own terms and privacy policy. To deliver a license and handle support, refunds, disputes, or fraud prevention, we may receive and retain limited order information such as:

  • Your purchase email and the order or payment identifier.
  • The product, amount, currency, order status, and purchase time supplied by the sales channel.
  • Refund or dispute status when applicable.

We do not receive your full card or bank account details from the sales channel.

5. Data Retention and Portability

Your trading data is stored locally and persists for as long as the library remains on your computer. You can remove individual sessions and trades within the app, or remove the complete library yourself.

For portability, the app provides an export tool at Settings → Data → Backup → Export that produces a single portable SQLite file containing every row of your local database (sessions, imported trades, journal notes, settings, and indicators). You can keep this file as a backup or move it to another device. The backup file is yours; we do not receive a copy.

License and order records are retained for as long as reasonably necessary to deliver the purchased license, enforce device limits, provide support, prevent abuse, resolve payment disputes, and meet applicable accounting or legal obligations.

6. Children's Privacy

TradeReplay is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the app, please contact us so we can confirm that the relevant data lives entirely on the child's device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.

7. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, the access, deletion, and portability rights granted under regulations such as the GDPR (European Union) and the CCPA (California) apply primarily to data you keep on your device, which you control directly through the app. Specifically, you may at any time:

  • Delete individual sessions, trades, notes, screenshots, or imported journals from within the app.
  • Remove your stored AI API key from Settings, instantly disabling all third-party AI calls.
  • Export the entire local database via Settings → Data → Backup → Export.
  • Remove the TradeReplay library from your computer after making any backup you wish to keep.
  • Contact us to ask about correction or deletion of purchase and license information, subject to applicable legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, and license-delivery requirements.

8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's features. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Significant changes may also be communicated through an in-app notice on first launch after the update.

9. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or about how the app handles your data, please contact us at zclk9000@me.com. For purchase or billing concerns, include the sales channel and order identifier, but never send passwords, payment-card details, or complete private API keys.
